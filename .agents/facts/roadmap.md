# Roadmap facts

- Active index: `docs/dev/roadmap.md`.
- Item briefs: `docs/dev/roadmap-items/<kebab-id>.md`.
- IDs are short, unique, descriptive kebab-case text rather than phase numbers.
- Active statuses are `Pending`, `Partial`, and `Blocked`.
- Execution follows declared dependencies; the radial visualisation must not be
  displaced by secondary reporting.
- Remove completed items from the active index after their durable behavior,
  decisions, validation, and release impact have moved to long-lived docs.
- Preserve a completed brief only when it still explains unresolved or deferred
  successor work.
